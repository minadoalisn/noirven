# Noirven

Noirven, also presented as Noirven 诺梵高奢, is a private high-jewelry brand and website for one-of-one numbered works. Each public work has a unique N serial, a physical product image, a story-series context, material and craft lines, a fixed ownership price, and a status of available or sold.

## Buying Rule

Noirven uses a one-work, one-serial, one-physical-object, one-owner rule. A collector is registered only after payment confirmation. Sold works remain publicly readable in the archive, while payment proof, account, and admin actions remain protected.
