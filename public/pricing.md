# Noirven Pricing And Ownership Terms

Last updated: 2026-11-09
Canonical site: https://nvonly.com

## Quick Answer

Noirven sells one-of-one numbered high-jewelry works at fixed ownership prices while each work is available. The price is shown on the public product page, payment proof is manually reviewed, and ownership is registered to one collector only after confirmation.

## Purchase Model

- Model: one physical high-jewelry work per serial number.
- Availability: each work is either available or archived as sold.
- Price type: fixed ownership price, not bidding.
- Currency shown: USD.
- Payment rail: BEP-20 USDT on BNB Smart Chain when the site payment flow is used.
- Confirmation: manual admin review before owner registration.
- Reproduction rule: no copy, no reissue, no second production of the same numbered work.

## Chinese Summary

Noirven 诺梵高奢采用“一件作品、一个编号、一个实物、一个最终主人”的归属规则。每件作品在公开页面显示编号、标题、材料、工艺、价格、状态和故事线。付款不等于自动归属，后台确认到账后才登记拥有者并进入发货与售后档案。

## Risk Notes

- Noirven does not present high jewelry as guaranteed investment return.
- Buyers should verify the product URL, serial number, receiving address, payment network, transaction hash, and delivery proof.
- Appraisal, gemstone certificate, and brand archive are different evidence types and should not be treated as the same document.
